Financial Details
A dedicated view for answering the manager's money questions: what has already cashed, what is signed but not cashed yet, and which signed deals need follow-up before they become real revenue.
Cashing Revenue
Deals already moved to Cashing / Closed Won. This is the clearest money signal.
Signed Contract
Signed / Booking deals that are waiting to move into Cashing.
Pending to Cash
Expected value still before Cashing. Use it to prioritize collection and activation follow-up.
Signed Date
Shows when the agreement was signed so the manager can track aging.
Follow-up Status
Highlights delayed signed deals and keeps financial risk separate from the main dashboard.
